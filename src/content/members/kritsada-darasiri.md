  en: >-

    systems, machine learning models, simulation development, and smart navigation technologies.
    He holds a Bachelor's degree in Nautical Science from Kasetsart University, followed by
    seagoing and maritime-industry experience, before pursuing a Master's degree in Maritime
    Technology and Logistics and now a PhD in Applied Ocean Engineering under the WISE Program
    (World-leading Innovative & Smart Education) — a national initiative to cultivate AI
    professionals for the maritime industry — at Tokyo University of Marine Science and
    Technology. His research focuses on integrating data-driven and physics-based control
    methodologies for autonomous ship motion and maritime control systems.
